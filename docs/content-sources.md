## Discrepancies and older statements

- **Project name:** current page headings predominantly use “Swarnagiri”; some navigation and the original process diagram still say “Jonnagiri.” The redesigned text uses Swarnagiri and preserves the diagram’s original label in its caption.
- **Pilot capacity:** the processing page narrative states 2 tons/hour; the specification table states 3 TPH. Neither is promoted as a settled capacity. The detail page explicitly notes the discrepancy.
- **Production schedule:** the executive summary predicts full-scale production in Q2 2025. That is a past projection and does not establish whether commissioning has happened. The redesign does not present it as a current timetable or claim commercial production is operating.
- **Resources:** the summary cites 361,000 ounces while a resource table totals 365,205 ounces, alongside other potential-resource estimates. These figures are not promoted or combined into a new current resource/reserve statement.
- **Design versus output:** 1,000 t/day and 50 t/hour are labelled as main-plant design figures; 0.4 MTPA is described as published permitted mining capacity. None is represented as measured current output.
- **Contact person:** “Priya Sharma” remains a demo person as requested by the user. The published reception email and company address are real public contact details. No telephone number is published on the reviewed contact page, so the dummy number was removed.

## Inquiry behaviour

The form validates, lets visitors review and edit, and can open a `mailto:` draft addressed to the published reception email. The visitor's email application handles sending. The site does not automatically send email or submit to the original site's backend. A downloadable text copy is also available. Browser tests inspect the email link without opening or sending an email.
